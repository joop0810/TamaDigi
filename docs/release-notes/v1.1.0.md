# TamaDigi v1.1.0

- **Chinese.** Simplified Chinese is now a language in the settings (tap your name, SETTINGS, the language line, until it shows the Chinese name). Everything is translated: menus, battles, every Digimon, every attack, items and areas. The translation has not been checked by a native speaker yet, so corrections are welcome.
- The axis labels under an egg no longer sit on top of the egg's version hint.

**What you need to do:** install the firmware. Your save stays: leave "Erase device" unticked. The sprite packs on the SD card do not change.
